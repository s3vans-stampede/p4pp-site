THE DRIVE HOME

The drive home should not feel like an interrogation.

School sport has professionalised faster than anyone prepared parents for. Matches are filmed and analysed, trials start at thirteen, and specialisation arrives before most children have worked out what they enjoy. You are funding it, driving to it and watching all of it, with no framework for what good support looks like at fifteen as opposed to twelve.

So you improvise. You go into fixing mode in the car after a bad match because it is the only tool you have. What comes back is silence, or it goes up in flames. Neither of you wanted that, and neither of you knows how to stop it happening again next Saturday.

WHAT HOCKEY FOUNDATIONS IS

Five elements behind every good young player. Your role in each one.

It starts with a gap. You and your child score all five separately, without seeing each other's answers, then look at the difference together. Most parents find in the first week that they have been solving the wrong problem.

Straight answers.

My child sent me this. What is it?
A six-week live course for you, the parent, run on Zoom in the evenings by an Olympian who coaches and parents in the same school system. Each week your child scores a short worksheet and you score the same one. The course works on the gap between the two. It is for parents of hockey players aged 12 to 18.
When does it start?
Tuesday 20 October 2026 at 19:30, South African time. Then every Tuesday to 24 November: six sessions in all.
How long is each session?
One hour, once a week, for six weeks. Live on Zoom in the evenings, South African time.
Do I need to know hockey?
No. Most parents on the course do not play. You learn enough to be useful at the dinner table, not enough to coach.
What if we miss a session?
Every session is recorded and the replay goes up the same night. The worksheets and the Blueprint review do not depend on being there live.
Who is this not for?
Parents looking for someone else to fix their child. The work is yours. It is also not for players under 12; the worksheets assume a child who can score themselves honestly.
